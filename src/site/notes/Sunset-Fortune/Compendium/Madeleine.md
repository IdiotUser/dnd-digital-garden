# History

As a child, Jean would often bring Madeline to a runied tour-fuie (self-standing dovecote) on the grounds of the estate.  Madeline implored their father, Silvére, to restore the building, unawares of the social status and regulations associate with dovecotes in medieval France-er... La Váler.  Eventually, Silvére makes a request of his overlord, who grants him the privilege.  Silvére is reminded of the small dovecote of his childhood, and spends time regularly at the tour-fuie, where he instills a care of pigeons in his children.
