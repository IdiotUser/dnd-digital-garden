A shepherd’s crook once hung above the door of The Crook house.  It was built on an old stockyard, serving as a respite for the herders who brought their livestock in to market from far afield.  The stockyard had since been redeveloped into housing estates, while the original row retained the name of Shambles street.  


At one point, the staff went missing from above the door, the publican not bothering to replace it.  Now the two chains that one suspended it dangle aimlessly from a wrought iron bracket.
