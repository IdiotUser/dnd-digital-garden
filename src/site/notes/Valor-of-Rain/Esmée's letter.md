> **Salutations and good fortunes.
> 
> I greet you as an ally, though that can quickly change depending on what you think is righteous. Be that as it may, the urgency that caused me to inscribe this is of your showstopper performance at the docks. The precision and agility of your flight is exactly what my team needs, and your background shows you have little quarrel with law. 
> 
> [[The Chaser\|The Chaser]] was once a proud property of one of my good friends, Izek, before he was murdered by the [[Valor-of-Rain/Maison de Ambre\|crown]]. Now that he's passed, the vessel was to be given to me, before you took it for yourself. However I can put that fact behind me for the cause of [[Valor-of-Rain/Valonier\|La Valér]]. 
> 
> I come to you with a proposition; accept a job with my crew, and I shall allow you to keep owning The Chaser, and grant additional gold pay from the rewards. If you accept, return to the [[Cracked Gull\|Cracked Gull]] tavern and speak the code “Through the dark, sly and keen” to the man with the red moustache. 
> 
> I shall see you soon, if not, consider your days numbered. I hope to form a strong professional relationship with your likes.
> 
> To Victory, to Destiny,
> [[Valor-of-Rain/Victorio\|Victorio]]
