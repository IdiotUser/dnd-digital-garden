## "Cuirasse" (Military police)
Officially, the archers du prévôt (lit. archers of the provost) , colloquially the Cuirasses  (lit. breastplates) serve as armed police in Valonier. 

The Cuirasses are so named for the distinctive white (unadorned steel plate) double (plate of breast and back) cuirass with brass sheathing (copper for officers)

The Cuirasses are each attached to an administrative region (the smallest of which being the *Particuliers*), each headed by a *Prévôt*, who reports to the provost of their greater region, up until the Provost General.

In response to the recent failed populist coup, the Cuirasses were given the power to enact justice on all peoples within Valonier, not just military personnel.  "*Deployed alongside newly arrived Mind Magi from the Oligarchy, nominally to protect the people from* 'political descent', *these are troubled times indeed*." ~ The *Anonymous Citizen*, a known co-conspiracy publicist.  This has put them at odds with the existing national police force *Gendarmerie Populaire* (lit. people's men-at-arms), who do not possess the extra judicial powers of the Cuirasses.
